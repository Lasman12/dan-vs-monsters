# Dan vs. The Monsters 👊

A pixel-art platformer. Dan doesn't need a reason — he just wants to beat up every monster. Because he feels like it.

**Play:** https://lasman12.github.io/dan-vs-monsters/ · **Android app:** [download the APK](https://github.com/Lasman12/dan-vs-monsters/releases/latest/download/dan-vs-monsters.apk)

English by default — Spanish and Hebrew in Settings. Progress is saved automatically on each device (no account needed).

## What's inside
- **2 worlds, 20 levels.** Every level has its own twist: ambushes, 3 hidden treasure chests, darkness with torches, a hazard wall that chases you, wind gusts, ice, conveyor belts, cannons, red/blue switch blocks (by crystal or to a beat), bounce mushrooms, falling rocks and geysers.
- **18 kinds of monsters**, plus golden elite versions: bombers, spear throwers, healing shamans, ground-pounding brutes, teleporting imps, fire spirits, crystal bats and more.
- **20 bosses, each with its own arena and signature moves** — webs and ceiling drops, burrowing, flooding arenas, freezing breath, shield-and-counter knights, lightning storms, lasers and homing rockets, tentacles, clones, splitting blobs, gravity flips and black holes. Bosses punish button-mashing.
- **Upgrades** with a big **i** on every card that shows an animation of *now* vs. *after the upgrade*.
- **World 2 unlocks the Weaponsmith** (bow, Minecraft-mace-style hammer, Thunder and Sharpness enchantments) and **Specials** (fireball, thunder storm, bubble shield, time warp, healing).
- Medals (bronze / silver / gold) for every level.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← → / A D | Move |
| Space / W / ↑ | Jump |
| J / X | Attack (↓ + J in the air = strike down) |
| K / Shift | Dash |
| Q | Potion |
| R | Switch weapon (World 2) |
| U | Special (World 2) |
| Esc | Pause · M sound |

On phones: joystick on the left, buttons on the right.
